## About

I am a data professional with hands-on experience across **data engineering, analytics, and business intelligence**. My work sits at the intersection of building dependable data foundations and making the resulting information useful to decision-makers.

I work with data from ingestion and transformation through modelling, analysis, and visualization. I have built API-driven data workflows, automated reporting pipelines, SQL-based analytical solutions, and interactive Power BI dashboards. I also bring experience coordinating analytics delivery and working with cross-functional teams.

I care about more than getting a pipeline to run or a dashboard to look good: I focus on data quality, maintainability, clear business questions, and outputs that people can act on.
